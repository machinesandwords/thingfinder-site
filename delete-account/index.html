 Privacy Policy

Delete your account

Two ways to remove your ThingFinder account and all associated data.

In the app

Open ThingFinder, go to Account, and select Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated data, including saved items and photos.

Without the app

Email support@thingfinder.app from the address associated with your account and request deletion. We'll process your request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes your profile, saved items, uploaded photos, and any other data associated with your account from our systems.
